TOOL 10Pull / junction box sizing

NEC 314.28(A)

Minimum box dimensions for raceways containing 4 AWG and larger conductors.

Min box dimension—
Rule applied—
Straight pull: length ≥ 8 × trade diameter of the largest raceway. Angle/U pull: distance from entry to opposite wall ≥ 6 × largest raceway + sum of other raceways on the same wall; entries with the same conductor ≥ 6 × apart. For combinations, size each direction per its pull type.
